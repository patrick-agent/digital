## Summary

- `site:tachy.io.vn` dao dong `55 -> 30` khong du de ket luan site bi mat index that.
- Production hien tai khong co dau hieu technical deindex hang loat: `robots`, `canonical`, `meta robots`, `sitemap` deu hop le.

## How To Read This File

- `Keep`: URL nen giu lam bai chinh.

   - copy phan noi dung huu ich tu bai se xoa vao pillar
   - cap nhat title, H2, FAQ, internal links trong pillar
4. Sau khi 301:
   - cap nhat internal links trong blog
   - submit lai pillar URLs trong Google Search Console
5. Theo doi 2-4 tuan:
   - `Indexed`
   - `Crawled - currently not indexed`
   - `Duplicate, Google chose different canonical`

## Rules For Choosing 301 vs Canonical

- Dung `301` khi:
  - title/intent trung nhau
  - content overlap lon
  - khong co gia tri rieng de rank doc lap

- Dung `canonical` tam thoi khi:
  - chua the merge ngay
  - van can giu URL song song vi ly do van hanh

- Giu rieng khi:
  - intent he hon va khac ro rang
  - co the rewrite de target query rieng

## Main Takeaway

- Van de lon nhat cua site hien tai khong phai crawlability ma la quality consolidation.
- Google co xu huong chi index mot phan khi nhieu bai moi cung nham vao mot cum keyword gan nhu giong nhau.
- Neu phai chon mot viec de lam ngay, hay gop cluster `VST mien phi`, `FL Studio cho nguoi moi`, `beat co ban`, `Kontakt add library`, `mixing co ban`, `EQ`, `compressor`, `reverb`, `delay` truoc.
